Shared locked system: one upright 2:3 full-bleed raster face; near-black `#0b0d0d`, muted antique gold `#a88952`, warm ivory `#ead9b2`, and at most three tiny dusty-rose `#b88478` star dots; flat paper/ink screen-print texture; thin rounded double border; restrained centered emblem; generous negative space and quiet lower field.
