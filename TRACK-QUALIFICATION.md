# Track qualification: Apps for your life

## Verdict

**Qualified.** The Celestial Archive is a consumer creativity and personal-reflection application for everyday use. It belongs in the **Apps for your life** track without adding an unrelated feature.

## Consumer use case

People can use the app to frame an everyday question, draw and reveal three cards, learn each named card's symbolism, receive a bilingual reflective synthesis, optionally export a designed image report, and explicitly save selected sessions to a private local archive. The Arcana and Grimoire also make it a creative learning and journaling tool rather than a one-time visual demo.

## Track alignment

- **Everyday life:** repeatable private reflection sessions for personal situations and creative journaling.
- **Creativity:** original interactive tarot deck, visual reports, card study, and symbolic interpretation.
- **Consumer app:** responsive pointer, touch, keyboard, and optional local camera-gesture interaction across desktop, phone, and tablet targets.
- **Personal ownership:** local-first operation; no reading is saved unless the user explicitly chooses Save.
- **Responsible boundaries:** the product is reflective entertainment and creative guidance, not medical, legal, financial, crisis, or deterministic fortune-telling advice.
